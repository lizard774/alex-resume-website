Thread: Cinematic Monolith
Register: Generous
Mode: Dark-only

Aesthetic Direction:
An editorial, high-contrast dark theme that feels like a premium magazine feature or a modern film credit sequence. The design relies on deep, cool slate-ink backgrounds contrasted with warm, metallic gold accents to create a sophisticated focal point. 

Typography & Scale:
Headings use a sharp, high-fashion serif (Instrument Serif) to provide dramatic optical personality, paired with a highly structured, legible sans-serif (IBM Plex Sans) for body copy and technical details. The typographic scale should be confident, utilizing oversized section labels and generous line heights.

Surfaces & Materials:
Surfaces are distinguished by very subtle lightness steps (deep ink to slightly lighter ink) and ultra-fine, low-contrast borders rather than heavy drop shadows. Corners are kept sharp or only very slightly rounded to maintain a structured, monolithic feel. 

Layout & Composition:
Whitespace is used as a structural element. The layout should favor asymmetric hero blocks, precise vertical rhythm, and clear categorical distinction through typographic weight and position rather than a rainbow of accent colors. Interactive states are quiet—subtle opacity shifts and gentle glows rather than dramatic movement.
